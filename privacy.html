Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 10, 2026

Overview

VoicePilot ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

By using the VoicePilot extension, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Local-First Architecture: VoicePilot is designed to operate locally on your device as much as possible. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any centralized servers owned by us.

However, to provide its core functionality, VoicePilot processes the following:

  • API Keys: You provide your own API keys (e.g., OpenRouter, ElevenLabs, Firecrawl) in the extension's settings. These are stored securely on your device using Chrome's local storage. We never have access to your API keys.
  • Webpage Content: When activated, VoicePilot may temporarily read the content (text, DOM structure) of the active webpage you are browsing to assist with navigation and context understanding.
  • Audio Data (Voice Input): When using voice commands, the extension captures your microphone audio locally to process your requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Local Processing: All settings, preferences, and API keys remain strictly on your local device.
  • Third-Party APIs: To fulfill your requests, VoicePilot transmits specific data (such as transcribed voice input or snippets of webpage content) directly to the third-party AI services you configure:
    • OpenRouter / OpenAI: Used to understand your commands and determine webpage interactions.
    • ElevenLabs: Used for synthesizing spoken responses.
    • Firecrawl: Used for analyzing web pages.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. Data is only sent to the specific APIs you have configured, and those interactions are subject to the respective privacy policies of those service providers.

3. Permissions Justification

VoicePilot requires specific browser permissions to function properly:

  • activeTab & scripting: Needed to inject the voice overlay interface and read the current page's content for context-aware interactions.
  • storage: Required to save your API keys and configuration settings locally on your device.
  • tabs: Required to navigate to new pages or switch between open tabs on your command.
  • offscreen: Required to process audio input from your microphone efficiently in the background.
  • <all_urls> (Host Permission): Required so the assistant can interact with and understand any webpage you navigate to.

4. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Your API keys are kept entirely local and are only read by the extension during runtime to make direct requests to the necessary APIs. We strongly advise treating your API keys like passwords and using usage limits on your third-party API dashboards.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@voicepilot.com (please update this email if needed).
